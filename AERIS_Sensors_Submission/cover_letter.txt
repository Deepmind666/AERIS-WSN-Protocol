Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "AERIS: Environment-Aware Hierarchical Routing for Reliable Wireless Sensor Networks under Realistic Channel Conditions" for consideration for publication in Sensors, Section "Sensor Networks."

This manuscript presents AERIS, a lightweight hierarchical routing protocol that prioritizes packet delivery reliability across four heterogeneous indoor/outdoor channel environments. The key contributions are:

  (1) A modular routing design (Context-Adaptive Switching, Skeleton backbone, Gateway reinforcement) with explicit environment-scoped operation boundaries and auditable control variables;

  (2) A regime-separated evidence design that distinguishes core reliability reporting from stress and sensitivity diagnostics, with publication-tier sample sizes (n = 30 to n = 3200 per cell) and Holm-corrected significance testing;

  (3) Cross-platform NS-3 trend validation for AERIS versus LEACH under aligned environment taxonomy.

The manuscript emphasizes reproducible conclusions under explicit metric, environment, baseline, and sample-size definitions. All publication-tier results include provenance metadata. We believe this work is well-suited for the readership of Sensors.

We confirm that:

  - All authors have read and approved the submitted version.
  - The study did not involve human subjects or animals.
